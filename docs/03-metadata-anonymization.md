# 03. DICOM Metadata Anonymization Guide

> **Deployment status:** Client-side anonymization is enabled for this approved
> deployment. It does not establish PS3.15 profile conformance or Clean Pixel Data;
> unsupported or unverified pixel processing fails closed and emits no such claim.

This document provides a technical and operational breakdown of the DICOM metadata anonymization engine within the OHIF Download Manager, detailing tag handling policies, built-in privacy profiles, custom whitelisting, and PHI exposure warnings.

---

## 1. Anonymization Architecture & Compliance Context

The metadata anonymizer (`anonymizer.js`) operates on client-side DICOM datasets prior to storage writing. It implements standard DICOM tag transformations derived from the **DICOM Standard PS 3.15 Annex E** (*Basic Application Level Confidentiality Profile*), drawing functional design rules from established tools such as DicomCleaner™ and RSNA MIRC Anonymizer.

## 2. Built-in Privacy Presets

The Download Manager includes four pre-configured anonymization presets to suit clinical research and dataset sharing needs:

![Figure 3.2: Anonymization Presets Toolbar](placeholder_anonymizer_presets.png)  
*Figure 3.2: One-click anonymization presets bar located at the top of the Anonymizer panel.*

| Preset Name | ID | Description | Default Risk Level |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **🛡️ Full Anonymization** | `strict` | **Strict Default**. Fully replaces patient identifiers, remaps all UIDs, shifts dates per patient, removes clinical text, deletes all private vendor tags, and strips physician names. | **Minimal Risk** |
| **🔬 Research Profile** | `research` | Replaces patient identifiers and shifts dates, but retains technical acquisition parameters (kVp, slice thickness), series descriptions, protocol names, and safe scanner vendor tags. | **Low Risk** |
| **📅 Keep Dates & Clinical Text** | `retain_dates` | Replaces primary patient ID/Name and remaps UIDs, but retains original study dates, acquisition times, clinical study descriptions, and physician names. | **Moderate Risk** |
| **⚠️ Minimal Anonymization** | `preserve_all` | Overrides Patient Name and ID only. Preserves true DICOM UIDs, original dates, equipment serial numbers, hospital identity, and ALL private vendor tags. | **HIGH PHI RISK** |

## 3. Detailed Tabbed Configuration Breakdown

When anonymization is enabled, settings are organized across five functional tabs:

![Figure 3.3: Tabbed Anonymizer Configuration Interface](placeholder_anonymizer_tabs.png)  
*Figure 3.3: Tabbed interface for granular control over patient identifiers, dates, descriptors, vendor tags, and advanced policies.*

---

### Tab 1: Patient & Identifiers

Allows explicit string replacements for primary patient demographic fields and UID generation:

| Setting Field | DICOM Tag | Target VR | Default Value | Description / Behavior |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Replacement Patient Name** | `(0010,0010)` | `PN` | `ANONYMOUS` | Overrides original patient full name string. |
| **Replacement Patient ID** | `(0010,0020)` | `LO` | `ANON1234` | Overrides medical record number / patient ID. |
| **Replacement Birth Date** | `(0010,0030)` | `DA` | `""` (Blank) | Optional fixed replacement date (`YYYYMMDD`). |
| **Replacement Patient Sex** | `(0010,0040)` | `CS` | `""` (Blank) | Replaces with `M`, `F`, `O`, or clears attribute. |
| **Replacement Accession Number** | `(0008,0050)` | `SH` | `""` (Blank) | Overrides order/accession tracking ID. |
| **Replacement Study Description** | `(0008,1030)` | `LO` | `""` (Blank) | Overrides study clinical description text. |
| **Replacement Series Description** | `(0008,103E)` | `LO` | `""` (Blank) | Overrides series description text. |
| **Generate New DICOM UIDs** | Multi-Tag | `UI` | `true` | Remaps StudyInstanceUID, SeriesInstanceUID, SOPInstanceUID, FrameOfReferenceUID to deterministic `2.25.xxx` UUIDs. |

> [!WARNING]
> Disabling **Generate New DICOM UIDs** (`remapUids: false`) preserves original DICOM UIDs. UIDs generated by PACS hardware frequently embed hidden timestamps, MAC addresses, or serial numbers that can be cross-referenced to identify origin facilities or patients.

---

### Tab 2: Dates & Times

Controls date obfuscation and clock time removal:

* **Keep original study dates** (`keepDates`):
  * **Unchecked (Default)**: Shifts all study dates (`DA` attributes) by a consistent random offset calculated deterministically per patient ID. Temporal relationships between longitudinal studies are preserved while concealing actual acquisition calendar dates.
  * **Checked**: Preserves true original calendar dates.
* **Keep exact clock times** (`keepExactTimes`):
  * **Unchecked (Default)**: Removes or zeros out exact time attributes (`TM` VRs, e.g., `AcquisitionTime`, `StudyTime`, `ContentTime`) to prevent cross-referencing against facility electronic health record (EHR) audit logs.
  * **Checked**: Retains true acquisition timestamps down to the second.

---

### Tab 3: Clinical & Descriptors

Fine-tunes clinical, technical, and patient physical attribute retention:

* **Keep all study & series descriptions and protocol names** (`keepDescriptors`): Retains text descriptions of procedures and acquisition protocols.
* **Keep series descriptions only** (`keepSeriesDescriptors`): Retains pulse sequence names (e.g., `"Axial T2 FRFSE"`) while clearing study-level descriptions.
* **Keep physician and staff names** (`keepPhysicians`): Retains `ReferringPhysicianName` `(0008,0090)`, `PerformingPhysicianName` `(0008,1050)`, and `OperatorsName` `(0008,1070)`.
* **Keep technical imaging & scanner parameters** (`keepAcquisitionParameters`): Retains non-identifying technical fields: `KVP` `(0018,0600)`, `SliceThickness` `(0018,0050)`, `RepetitionTime` `(0018,0080)`, `EchoTime` `(0018,0081)`, `ContrastBolusAgent` `(0018,0010)`.
* **Keep graphic & ROI overlays** (`keepOverlays`): Retains vector graphics and ROI annotations stored in DICOM Group `60xx` overlay attributes.
* **Keep waveform & ECG curve data** (`keepCurves`): Retains physiological signals stored in DICOM Group `50xx`.
* **Age Grouping & Rounding**:
  * **Group ages over 89 as "90"** (`aggregateAgesOver89`): Enforces HIPAA Safe Harbor requirement aggregating all patient ages over 89 into a single category (`90Y`).
  * **Round patient age** (`roundAgeToYears`): Rounds patient age to nearest N years (default: `5` years).

---

### Tab 4: Vendor Tags & Custom Tag Whitelist

Manages private DICOM group tags and attribute-level exceptions:

![Figure 3.4: Vendor Tag Policy and Whitelist Interface](placeholder_tag_whitelist_tab.png)  
*Figure 3.4: Selection dropdown for Private Tag Policy and custom DICOM tag whitelist editor.*

#### Private Vendor Tags Policy (`privateTagsPolicy`)
Private tags are manufacturer-specific attributes defined in odd-numbered DICOM groups (e.g., GE, Siemens, Philips custom fields):
1. **Remove All Private Vendor Tags (`remove_all`) — Default / Safest**: Deletes all odd-group tags (`group % 2 !== 0`).
2. **Keep Safe Scanner Vendor Tags (`keep_safe`)**: Retains non-identifying quantitative private attributes essential for advanced post-processing (e.g., SUV body weight scale factors, CT radiation dose index, MR diffusion b-value tables).
3. **Keep ALL Private Vendor Tags (`keep_all`) — Unsafe**: Retains all private vendor tags without filtering. High risk of PHI leakage (e.g., raw technician notes or patient identifiers hidden in proprietary Siemens/GE shadow groups).

#### Custom Tag Whitelist
Allows users to specify explicit 8-character hexadecimal DICOM tags (e.g., `00100040` for Patient Sex or `0008103E` for Series Description) to be exempted from deletion. 

* **Quick Add Buttons**: Provides one-click buttons to append common tags.
* **Tag Preview**: Instantly parses and displays recognized tag names and VRs.

---

### Tab 5: Advanced Settings

Configures special object handling and equipment identity:

* **Structured Report (SR) Policy** (`structuredContentPolicy`):
  * **Remove (`remove`) — Default**: Strips SR text content items to eliminate free-text narrative PHI.
  * **Sanitize (`sanitize`)**: Retains SR structure while performing best-effort regex cleaning of detected patient names and dates in text strings.
* **Keep hospital & institution identity** (`keepInstitutionIdentity`): Retains `InstitutionName` `(0008,0080)`, `InstitutionAddress` `(0008,0081)`, and `InstitutionalDepartmentName` `(0008,1040)`.
* **Keep scanner & equipment identity** (`keepDeviceIdentity`): Retains `StationName` `(0008,1010)`, `DeviceSerialNumber` `(0018,1000)`, and `ManufacturerModelName` `(0008,1090)`.
* **Reject embedded document files** (`rejectEncapsulatedDocuments`): Blocks export of DICOM instances containing embedded PDF, CDA, or XML documents (`SOPClassUID = 1.2.840.10008.5.1.4.1.1.88.11` or `.59`).

## 4. Non-Default Warning Banner & PHI Exposure Alert

Whenever an anonymization profile deviates from strict defaults (e.g., preserving original dates, keeping physician names, or retaining private vendor tags), the Download Manager displays a prominent **Yellow Alert Banner**:

![Figure 3.5: Non-Default Settings Warning Banner](placeholder_anonymizer_warning_banner.png)  
*Figure 3.5: Warning indicator alerting the user that current custom settings expose potential PHI fields.*

* **Active PHI Risk Summary**: Lists every active setting that exposes potential PHI.
* **Restore Strict Defaults Button**: Single click action to immediately reset all configuration fields back to the strict `DEFAULT_ANONYMIZER_CONFIG`.
